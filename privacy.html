Privacy Policy

Draft dated October 11, 2026 · HuddleTape

HuddleTape (“we,” “us”) makes this website. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We wrote it in plain English on purpose.

The short version

  • This website collects only what you submit in the signup form.
  • We don’t sell your data, and we don’t share it for advertising.
  • HuddleTape is for adults 21 and older.

What we collect

Signup form. When you ask for early access, we receive your name, email address, the TV or streamer model if you give it, and your confirmation that you’re 21 or older. The form is delivered to our email through a form-forwarding service (FormSubmit), which processes it under its own privacy policy. This website does not collect anything else.

Support. If you email us, we keep the conversation so we can help you.

How we use it

  • To keep your place on the list and email you about HuddleTape. You can opt out anytime.
  • To answer support email.
  • To comply with the law and enforce our Terms.

Sharing

We share data only with service providers that help us run this website (for example form delivery and email), under terms that limit their use of it; when the law requires it; or as part of a merger or sale of the business, in which case this policy still applies to your data. We don’t sell personal information.

Retention

We keep signup info until you ask us to delete it. Support email is kept so we can help you.

Your choices and rights

  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct or delete it, by emailing huddletape.app@gmail.com.
  • Unsubscribe from list emails at any time.

Depending on where you live (for example Texas or California), you may have additional rights under state privacy law. We’ll honor them, and we won’t treat you differently for using them.

Age

HuddleTape is only for people 21 and older. We don’t knowingly collect information from anyone under 21. If you believe someone under 21 has given us information, contact us and we’ll delete it.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect information, but no system is perfectly secure.

Changes

We’ll update this page when our practices change and note the date at the top. For significant changes, we’ll give notice on this website or by email.
